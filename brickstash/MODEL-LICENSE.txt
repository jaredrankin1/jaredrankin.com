silueta.onnx is the "silueta" background-removal model distributed by rembg
(https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg), a compact retraining of U^2-Net
(https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net). Both projects are released under the
Apache License 2.0. It runs in the visitor's browser with ONNX Runtime Web
(MIT License); no photo is uploaded anywhere.
