## Top 3 takeaways per exec

### Elaine Park — CEO
1. **The grading preview is the product's signature moment.** Watching the figure scuff and yellow (`22-home-grade-adjusted-6_5.png`) is the one thing novices *and* Brandon the skeptic responded to. That's the brand: "see your fig's condition".

3. **We built more than we're showing.** A real back office with tables, reports, and scan already exists (`60-unlinked-web-inventory-desktop.png`), and nobody in the room could reach it. That's a framing problem before it's a build problem.

### Raj Mehta — CFO
1. **The people who'd pay can't do the paid jobs yet.** Sofia, Luis, Rob, Keisha, and Tom named $5–50/month or $10 per report, *conditional on* export, lots, listing, or bulk (findings M4). All five failed T6.
2. **The people who love it won't pay.** Dev, Tanya, and Aiko: $0 to a few dollars once. Delight without a revenue path is a marketing cost.
3. **The number is the product, and it isn't defensible yet** (C3). Index above new and cracks with no price effect mean pros won't stake money on it, and pros are the only ones paying.

### Grace Holloway — CTO
1. **Two bugs were really design decisions we never surfaced:** local-first Home with a manual Save (C1), and an 8-result search cap (M2). Both are cheap to fix compared with how confused they made people. C1 is first.
2. **The scoring is doing what we coded, and that's the problem** (C3). Crack severity barely touches the composite and doesn't cross a multiplier band. 3. **The host was fine today** (no cold starts, sub-2-second loads). Everything that hurt was product and front-end, not infrastructure. Quick tech fixes: the `/welcome/` 404 (M8), Market thumbnails (m1), serial search (m3). Also check tenant authorization on sequential item IDs, which we didn't probe.

### Andre Silva — CMO

2. **Copy to keep:** "Is any of this worth something?" (P2) as the consumer hook; "the only LEGO app that's fun" (P5); and, as a warning, "BrickLink with physics" (P9).

### Nina Brandt — COO

## Consensus (one paragraph)

BrickStash's distinctive asset is **visual condition grading tied to a market price**. It's the one moment that landed with novices and pros alike. But the current app breaks the chain at three points that matter more than any new feature: work done on Home doesn't reliably reach the account (C1), figures the camera can't name have no way forward (C2), and the price isn't defensible to anyone who knows the market (C3). Fix those three, connect the reseller back office that already exists, and put one sentence on the home screen saying what the app does. Then rerun this study **with real people** (stores and resellers first, consumers second) before choosing a segment or a price. Everything here is a simulated hypothesis until real users confirm it.

## Prioritized next steps

### Product (UX)
1. **C1** Sync Home items to the account on add (or an obvious "Save 3 to Inventory" with an unsaved badge). Make the Account copy truthful.
2. **C2** Add "Find it in the catalogue" on unnamed fig cards, keep the user's photo, and never drop figures silently on Save.
3. **M1** Add completeness, authenticity ("suspected fake" → no price), custom/mixed, and sealed fields.
4. **M3** One-line value proposition, a labelled "+ Add a fig", and a sample stash on first run.
5. **M5** Prefill the graded price and make "priced" move items to In stock; use real dates.
6. **M7** Accessibility pass: 14 px minimum, inline errors, sticky Save in the grade sheet, labelled icons.

### Tech
1. **C3** Grade→price function: a crack ladder that moves price, a cap or explanation against the new price, unset slider defaults, and source/date/sample size on the card. Examine sw0218's index-over-new case.
2. **M2** Search: pagination beyond 8, token-order-independent matching, synonyms.
3. **M8** Serve `/welcome/` (reload 404). **m1** Market thumbnails. **m3** Index BC serials.
4. Check tenant authorization on `/api/mobile/item/<id>` (not tested). Gate `?bsdebug`. Serve the ONNX model from the app's own origin. Remove the leftover résumé markup.
5. **M4 (bridge)** Link the existing desktop back office (`/inventory`, Reports, Quick add) from the app on desktop.

### Go-to-market
1. **Don't market valuations to pros** until C3 is fixed. Lead with "see your fig's condition" (the grading preview), not "know what it's worth".
2. **Recruit 5–8 real users per segment** (stores/consignment, resellers, insurance-minded collectors, parents/inheritors) and rerun T1, T2, T4, and T5 with real figures and real photos. See `limitations.md` for the questions to settle.
3. **Price-test only conditional offers**, e.g. a per-report insurance PDF ($10, from P6) or a store tier with lots and Storefront ($30–50, from P10), and only after those features exist.
4. Retitle the Market lists ("Most valuable in the BrickCheck 300") and define MMi on screen before any public demo.
